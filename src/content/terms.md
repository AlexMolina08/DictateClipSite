## 1. What auge does

auge turns a video you record or import into a finished short‑form video. It
transcribes your narration, then an AI editorial agent designs word‑timed
captions and animated visual elements, which auge compiles and renders on your
iPhone. You can preview, lightly edit, and export the result. Some processing
(transcription and the editorial plan) happens in the cloud; the video itself is
rendered on your device and is never uploaded. See our
[Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.
